# FinPilot - AI Revenue Recovery System 🚀

## 🏆 Razorpay Buildathon 2026 - AI Revenue Recovery Track

### 📌 Problem Statement
Businesses lose billions in revenue due to overdue invoices, partial payments, and missed follow-ups. Manual reconciliation takes hours and is error-prone.

### 💡 Our Solution
FinPilot is an AI-powered revenue recovery system that automates the entire workflow:
- **Reconciliation**: 3-Tier AI system (Rule → Groq → Claude)
- **Follow-ups**: AI-generated professional emails
- **Reply Tracking**: Auto-detection of customer replies
- **Payment Promises**: Smart detection and reminder scheduling
- **CA Reports**: Professional audit-ready exports

### 🎯 Key Features

#### 🤖 3-Tier AI Reconciliation
| Tier | Technology | Use Case | Cost |
|------|-----------|----------|------|
| 1 | Rule Match | Exact matches | Free |
| 2 | Groq AI | Partial payments | $0.001/record |
| 3 | Claude AI | Complex cases | $0.03/record |

#### 📧 Smart Follow-up System
- AI-generated professional emails
- Auto-detection of customer replies
- Payment promise detection ("I will pay on X")
- Auto-reminders on promised dates

#### 📊 CA-Ready Reports
- Executive Summary
- Status Breakdown
- AI Tier Analysis
- Export: Excel, CSV, JSON

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Recharts
- **Database**: MySQL
- **AI**: Groq API, Claude API
- **Email**: Gmail API, Nodemailer
